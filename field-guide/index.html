The Capacity Wheel Field Guide

You can keep pushing through. The more useful question is what it is costing you.

Your Capacity Wheel shows whether the way you are working and living right now is building your capacity or using it up.

Maybe you already know something is off.

You wake up tired after enough time in bed. Your energy disappears by mid-afternoon. You need more caffeine to get the same output. You are less patient than you want to be. Or everything still looks fine from the outside, but it takes more effort than it used to.

That is the part worth paying attention to.

Because the problem is rarely that you suddenly cannot perform.

It is that performing starts costing you more.

The Wheel helps you see where that may be happening, what you can change yourself, and when it is worth looking underneath the surface instead of continuing to guess.

What do we mean by capacity?

Capacity is what you have available to meet the demands of your life.

It is the energy to get through the day without crawling over the line.

The ability to think clearly when the decision matters.

The ability to stay steady when the room is not.

The ability to recover well enough to do it again tomorrow.

We tend to treat those things as separate.

We don’t.

Clockwise builds
Anticlockwise erodes

Your biology affects how you think and lead.

How you lead affects what you do.

What you repeatedly do shapes the conditions around you.

Those conditions feed back into your biology.

That is the Capacity Wheel.

The question is whether it is currently working with you or against you.

Your score is a signal, not a grade

Your score out of 100 summarises how you answered the sixteen questions.

A higher score means more of what you described is currently supporting your capacity. A lower score means more of it is creating drag.

  • It does not mean you are operating at 62% of your potential.
  • It does not diagnose your health.
  • And there is little value in comparing your score with somebody else’s.

The useful comparison is you against you.

  • What does your Wheel look like now?
  • What happens when you change something?
  • What happens when the load changes?

That is where the value starts.

What your state is telling you

Peak

You have something in reserve.

Your current way of operating is supporting the load you are carrying. The temptation here is to assume you have solved it.

A better question is:

What is working that you need to protect?

Because capacity tends to disappear when the load rises, not when life is easy.

Steady

You are holding, but there is not much to spare.

Things are mostly working. The issue is margin.

A harder quarter, disrupted sleep, more travel, pressure at home or an unexpected problem can quickly consume what is left. This is the time to build reserve before you need it.

Conserve

You are spending capacity faster than you are rebuilding it.

You may still be performing well. That is precisely why this state is easy to ignore.

The work still gets done. People still rely on you. You can still push when required. But more of your own energy is being used to produce the same result. Leave that equation unchanged for long enough and it becomes your new normal.

Restore

The current equation is asking for more than it gives back.

This is not a sign that you need more discipline. If pushing harder were going to fix it, it probably would have by now. The priority is to work out what is taking so much out of you, reduce the unnecessary load, and start rebuilding.

There are two places to start

You can influence every part of the Wheel, but two parts can be changed directly.

Behaviour, the point at the bottom of the wheel

This is what you actually do.

Your sleep habits. Movement. Fuelling. Recovery. Caffeine. Training. The phone you pick up when you finally have ten quiet minutes.

Not what you know you should do.

What actually happens.

Environment, the point on the left of the wheel

These are the conditions around you.

Your calendar. Meetings. Travel. Notifications. Workload. The people who can reach you. The space you have to think and recover.

This matters because sometimes we ask an already stretched person to become more disciplined inside an environment that keeps working against them.

That is a hard way to solve the problem.

Before you buy anything from us, change the obvious thing.

If your Wheel has shown you something you already know, start there.

  • If your sleep window has disappeared, protect it.
  • If you have no space to think, make some.
  • If you know the fourth coffee is keeping the cycle going, test changing it.
  • If your phone owns every empty minute, reclaim one of them.

Do one thing, not seven.

Then notice what happens.

If that solves the problem, good.

You may not need us.

The expensive part is when you don’t know what to change.

This is where people start wasting time.

  • You sleep eight hours and still wake tired.
  • You cut the afternoon coffee. Nothing obvious changes.
  • You take magnesium.
  • You stop screens before bed.
  • You exercise more. Then less.
  • You try the advice your wearable gives you.

Eventually, one of two things happens.

You keep changing things without knowing what worked.

or

You stop trying because nothing seems to make enough difference.

Meanwhile, you adapt.

You get better at compensating for being tired.

You use caffeine, urgency or willpower.

The lower-energy afternoon starts feeling normal.

You stop remembering what properly restored felt like.

And the cost is bigger than feeling tired.

It can show up in your patience, training, concentration, decision-making and how much energy is left when you get home.

The cost is not one bad score. It is months of guessing while the same pattern keeps running.

That is where measurement becomes useful.

Start with the data you already have

Your Oura or WHOOP is collecting more than a score.

If you wear an Oura Ring or WHOOP, there is a good chance you already have weeks or months of useful information sitting there.

  • Sleep timing
  • Resting heart rate
  • HRV
  • Recovery
  • Patterns across better nights and worse ones

Your wearable is very good at showing you what happened.

The harder question is:

Why?

  • Was it the late workout?
  • The afternoon coffee?
  • Alcohol?
  • Workload?
  • Timing?
  • Recovery?
  • Several small things stacking on top of one another?

That distinction matters. Because changing five things at once may improve your score, but it does not teach you which thing mattered. And if nothing changes, you still do not know why.

Why sleep first?

Because sleep is one of the easiest places to see the Capacity Wheel come together.

Your behaviour can show up there.

Your environment can show up there.

Your current biological state can show up there.

And what happens overnight changes how much you have available the next day.

It gives us a useful first layer without immediately asking you to undergo a battery of testing.

Sometimes that layer is enough.

If it is, stop there.

What if it isn’t just sleep?

Sometimes we find something useful in the sleep data and the next move is obvious.

Sometimes the data raises a bigger question.

  • Why is your energy still falling even when sleep improves?
  • Why does food appear to change the way you feel so dramatically?
  • Why are you doing the sensible things but recovering differently from the person next to you?

That is when it may be worth measuring another layer.

We don’t start with every test we know how to run. We add measurement when the previous layer leaves an important question unanswered.

That matters to us. More data is not automatically more useful. The right data should reduce uncertainty and make the next decision easier.

A note on when a data review is not enough

The Sleep Score Review helps you understand patterns in your sleep and recovery data. It is not medical diagnosis or treatment.

If you snore loudly, someone has noticed that you stop breathing while asleep, or you regularly fall asleep during the day, speak with your GP first. Those symptoms deserve proper medical assessment rather than a wearable-data review.

What happens to your data?

We use your data only to complete your review. We store it securely and delete it within 90 days, unless you specifically ask us to keep it. We do not sell or share it.
